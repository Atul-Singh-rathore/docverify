#  DocuVerify (Document Authentication & Regulatory Compliance System)

## What is DocuVerify?
It is an intelligent web application designed to automatically verify official documents such as compliance certificates, identity cards, and academic records to check whether they are authentic or tampered with.

## Problems It Solves
* **Automated Verification:** It is an intelligent web application designed to automatically verify documents such as compliance certificates and identity cards.
* **Document Fraud:** Stops people from using fake, edited, or forged official papers.
* **Slow Manual Checks:** Eliminates tedious manual inspections of seals, signatures, and layouts, saving hours of manual work.
